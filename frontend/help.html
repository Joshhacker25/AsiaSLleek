Help & support

Content rules

Adults only. Prohibited: anyone under 18 or of uncertain age, non-consensual content, hidden-camera content, impersonation, trafficking, stolen material, and any other unlawful material. Every upload stays private until a moderator approves it.

Report content

Use the Report button on any video page, or submit a report here. Reports are reviewed by moderators.

Request removal

If content shows you without consent, or infringes your rights, request removal. Provide the video ID and your email so the team can contact you.

Appeals

If your video or report decision was wrong, submit an appeal from your account. Appeals are reviewed by a moderator who was not the original reviewer where possible.
